Ounass: Ready for the Moment

A conceptual campaign that turns Ounass's two-hour delivery into the story: last-minute plans, a quick order, and arriving ready.

Conceptual proposal. Group project of five, MKT 306 Social Media Advertising, Canadian University Dubai, 2025-26. Ounass did not commission this and has not used it. All budgets and targets are proposals. All campaign visuals are AI-generated concept mockups that I created, not real posts, and carry no real results.

The idea in four beats

Dubai plans change quickly. The content follows one story so the two-hour promise is shown, not just stated.

An invitation arrives
A last-minute plan.
The order
Quick purchase in the Ounass app, with AI outfit suggestions.
Delivery
The look arrives within two hours.
Ready
She arrives confident.

Content concepts

Last-Minute Dinner. An urgent invitation, an AI-picked outfit, a stylish arrival.

Spontaneous Beach Club. An unexpected invite, a quick order, the right outfit on time.

"I have nothing to wear to X." Scrolling AI outfit picks, then delivery and the final look.

TikTok. "POV: you have two hours to get ready" and get-ready-with-me videos, with influencers and users sharing under #ReadyForTheMoment.

Platforms. Instagram takes 60% of ad spend for cinematic Reels and Collection Ads. TikTok takes 40% for Spark Ads on the best influencer content.

Timing. Higher bids from 4 to 9 pm, Thursday to Saturday, when spontaneous plans peak. People who engage on Instagram but do not install the app are retargeted on TikTok with a "Two-Hour Challenge" video.

The strategy in summary

The proposal builds the whole campaign around one promise, two-hour luxury delivery. Instagram carries the aspirational storytelling, TikTok drives reach and participation, and influencer and user content shows the promise in real moments. Paid spend amplifies the strongest content, and the Ounass app is where it converts, with every part tied to a measurable target.

Key takeaway: a luxury social campaign works best when the story, platforms, budget and measures all serve a single clear idea. This remains a conceptual proposal and was never launched.

What this shows

Skills used: content strategy, campaign storytelling, budget allocation, media planning, KPI design.

This project helped me explore how a luxury brand can develop a social media campaign that feels consistent with its identity while still connecting with its target audience. I learned how audience insights, platform selection, content planning, budgeting, and campaign objectives need to work together rather than be treated as separate decisions. Developing the proposal also helped me think more carefully about how creative ideas can be translated into a structured marketing plan, with measurable objectives and a clear rationale behind each decision.
